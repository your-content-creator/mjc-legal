Privacy policy

MJC Upload Agent · Effective 12 November 2026

Who we are

MJ Creative LLC ("MJ Creative", "we") runs the MJC Upload Agent, an internal tool that uses the YouTube API Services.

YouTube

The MJC Upload Agent uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies to the data Google processes.

What we access

With your permission, the tool uses these Google permissions:

  • Upload YouTube videos: to upload and schedule the videos we produce for your channel, with their title, description, tags, thumbnail and publish time.
  • View your YouTube account: to confirm we are posting to the right channel and to check that a scheduled video landed.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports: to read your channel's performance (views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers) so we can report results to you.

What we store

A sign-in token for your channel, kept on MJ Creative's own computer in a folder only our account can read. We do not store your Google password. We keep a log of what we scheduled (date, video title, video link) and the analytics figures we report to you, in our internal work files.

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or share your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to train AI models. We use the permissions above only to post the videos you hired us to post and to report their results to you.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Email us and we delete your stored token and any data we hold about your channel within 7 days. When a client contract ends, we delete the token within 30 days.

Security

Tokens stay on one company computer protected by its user login. They are never put in shared drives, email or chat.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above.

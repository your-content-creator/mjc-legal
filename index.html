MJC Upload Agent

by MJ Creative

MJ Creative is a content agency. The MJC Upload Agent is our internal tool for scheduling short videos on the YouTube channels of clients who have asked us to manage their posting.

A channel owner or manager signs in once with Google and approves access. The tool then uploads the client's finished videos with their title, description and tags, and schedules them to publish at the agreed time. It can also read the channel's own analytics so we can report results back to the client.

The tool is not offered to the public.
